Scope

What the companion does, and does not, do.

Does

  • Arrives with your questions written down and asks them
  • Records instructions and dosages exactly as given
  • Reconciles the new prescription against every standing medicine
  • Flags what is unresolved and carries it to the next visit
  • Carries prior reports and results to the next doctor

Does not

  • Give medical advice or a second opinion
  • Change or suggest a dose
  • Overrule or argue with the treating doctor
  • Consent to procedures on the family's behalf
  • Share the record with anyone but the paying family
